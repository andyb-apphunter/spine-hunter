Spine Hunter privacy

What's stored: the books you add (titles, authors, dates, ratings, notes, genres and cover pictures), your trophies, yearly goal and settings.

Where: in your own private area of the app's Google Firebase database (Sydney region), linked to the Google account you sign in with, plus an offline copy on each device you use. Only you can read or change your journal: the database rules block everyone else, including other Spine Hunter users.

Sign-in: Google sign-in gives the app your name, email address and a user ID. Nothing else from your Google account is read.

Book lookups: when you search for a book or cover, the title, author or ISBN is sent to Open Library, Google Books and Apple Books to find details. Your notes and ratings are never sent.

No ads, no tracking, no selling. There are no analytics or advertising tools in the app.

Removing your data: delete your books in the app, or ask the app's owner to delete your account's data.
